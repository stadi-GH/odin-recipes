Odin Recipes

Description

Pilau, a well and highly rated meal among most of the households in my country. It is prepared mostly by experienced people since it needs a lot of patience and skill in its preparation.

The meal comes with a combination of a lot of ingredients, enriching it with a high nutrional value

ingredients

  • 500g of beef, cut into small pieces
  • 2 cups of basmati rice
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp of ginger paste
  • 1 tbsp of garlic paste
  • 1tbsp of tomato paste
  • 4 cups of beef broth/water
  • 3/8 cup of cooking oil/ghee
  • salte to taste
  • guacamoli for serving
steps
  1. prepare the spices, if they are whole grind or chop them
  2. boil the meat with salt and ginger at medium heat till its tender
  3. brown the onions in a cooking oil
  4. add the ginger and tomato paste in the onions and cook for a minute
  5. add your favorite spices in the onions
  6. add the meat in the onions, stir and allow it absorb the spices for 5 minutes
  7. add the liquid you have, broth or water and stir
  8. add rice in the pot and stir, let it cook till water reduces
  9. reduce the heat and allow the rice to cook till the water subsides
  10. serve the food alongside your favorite drink
